  <summary><span style="color:var(--global-theme-color)">Teaching philosophy and experience</span></summary>
<div style="text-align: justify">  
I am passionate about teaching courses in my area of expertise and have taken up various teaching roles whenever the opportunity arose.  I value teaching not only as a means of imparting knowledge but also getting students just as excited as I am about my areas of interest! Moreover, I’ve found that preparing to be a good instructor, answering student questions, and helping them navigate different concepts helps me gain deeper understanding about the subject area while exposing me to different points of view. 
<br/><br/>
During my time at Stanford, I’ve been involved in courses geared towards a variety of student levels — undergraduates, graduate students as well as working professionals. These courses involved theoretical material, simulation based exercises, and lab-based components. I’ve received overwhelmingly positive (anonymous) feedback from students and this has served as a constant source of motivation to keep improving as a teacher over the years. I was also awarded the 2022 James F. Gibbons Outstanding Student Teaching Award for receiving rave reviews from both students and faculty.
</div>
